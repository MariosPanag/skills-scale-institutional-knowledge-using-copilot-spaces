# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The QA/Testing Lead owns quality assurance strategy, test coverage standards, and acceptance testing for each release. They partner with Product Managers, Developers, and Project Managers to ensure features meet quality thresholds before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and approach for each project
- Establish and maintain testing standards and coverage expectations
- Review acceptance criteria for testability
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Triage and prioritize bugs and quality issues
- Run pre-release smoke tests and quality sign-off

### Goals
- Ensure releases meet quality expectations and customer needs
- Reduce production incidents through better test coverage
- Help the team build testable, reliable solutions

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria reviews
- Daily standups and test status updates
- Bug triage sessions and release readiness reviews

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers to validate implementation quality and test coverage
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and user-facing quality
- Supports Project Managers by reporting blockers, release readiness, and risk areas

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead provides architectural guidance, resolves complex technical dependencies, and mentors the engineering team. They help maintain technical quality and system health across the project.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and propose architectural improvements
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Resolve cross-team technical dependencies
- Mentor developers and support engineering decision-making
- Define coding standards and best practices
- Advocate for refactoring, maintainability, and technical debt reduction

### Goals
- Build a scalable, maintainable system
- Reduce technical risk and unplanned work
- Foster a strong engineering culture

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical deep dives
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Technical risk escalations
- Code review and mentorship

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with Developers to guide architecture and implementation choices
- Collaborates with Product Managers on feasibility, trade-offs, and delivery implications
- Partners with Project Managers on dependency management and sequencing of technical work

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
The Sponsor provides business context, strategic priorities, and escalation authority. They ensure the project is aligned with organizational goals and has the resources needed to succeed.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and success metrics
- Provide business justification and strategic context
- Secure resources, budget, and executive support
- Serve as escalation point for business-impacting decisions
- Communicate project outcomes to leadership stakeholders
- Resolve cross-project priority conflicts

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver demonstrable business value
- Maximize return on investment
- Align delivery with organization strategy

### Typical Communication
- Monthly status updates and milestone reviews
- Executive steering committee meetings
- Escalations from PM and Product leads on priority or resource decisions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Product Managers on business goals and success measures
- Provides authority and prioritization support to Project Managers
- Collaborates with leaders across teams to unblock constraints and business decisions

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master or Delivery Lead facilitates team execution, removes blockers, and helps the team improve working practices. They support the team's rhythm while keeping delivery focused and sustainable.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate ceremonies such as sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives
- Help remove team blockers and resolve workflow friction
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain healthy delivery flow and process discipline
- Support visibility into progress, risks, and dependencies

### Goals
- Improve team productivity and predictability
- Create a healthy, collaborative delivery environment
- Support consistent execution across iterations

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and retrospective sessions
- Team standups and daily coordination
- Delivery health updates and improvement discussions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Supports Developers by improving workflow and reducing interruptions
- Partners with Project Managers on delivery tracking and milestone planning
- Works with Product Managers to align priorities and maintain a clear backlog flow

---

## Infrastructure / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
The Infrastructure / DevOps Engineer manages deployment pipelines, infrastructure, monitoring, and operational readiness. They help ensure the team can release safely and reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure, environments, and configurations
- Configure monitoring, logging, and alerting
- Execute deployments and coordinate rollback plans
- Support security and compliance implementation in infrastructure
- Help enable incident response and operational readiness

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Maintain system uptime and performance
- Reduce deployment-related risks

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Infrastructure requirements in sprint planning
- On-call escalations and incident response

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers to support build and deployment readiness
- Coordinates release activities with Project Managers and QA
- Helps Product Managers understand operational trade-offs and release dependencies

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
The Security / Compliance Officer ensures the project meets security standards, manages risk, and supports compliance requirements across the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture and implementation for security risks
- Define security and compliance requirements
- Conduct threat assessments and security reviews
- Support secure configuration and deployment standards
- Ensure compliance obligations are understood and met
- Contribute to incident response and remediation planning

### Goals
- Reduce security exposure and operational risk
- Ensure compliance with policy and regulatory requirements
- Enable secure delivery without slowing the team unnecessarily

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and architecture discussions
- Risk and compliance checkpoints
- Security incident escalation and mitigation updates

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Developers on secure design and implementation practices
- Supports Project Managers and Sponsors on risk management and compliance impacts
- Coordinates with Infrastructure / DevOps for secure deployment and operations

---

## User Experience (UX) Designer

### Role Summary
The UX Designer defines user interactions, creates prototypes, and validates usability. They help ensure the product is intuitive, accessible, and effective for end users.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs and workflows
- Design interfaces, interactions, and user journeys
- Create prototypes and validate concepts with stakeholders or users
- Work with Product Managers to translate outcomes into customer experience improvements
- Collaborate with Developers and QA on usability and implementation quality

### Goals
- Improve usability, clarity, and customer satisfaction
- Reduce friction in the user journey
- Ensure product decisions are grounded in user needs

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery and concept reviews
- Design critiques and prototype walkthroughs
- Usability testing and validation sessions

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on customer needs and feature prioritization
- Works with Developers to ensure designs are implemented as intended
- Supports QA by clarifying expected user interactions and edge cases

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The roles complement one another: Product sets direction, Project coordinates execution, Developers build, QA validates quality, Technical Lead guides architecture, Security and Infrastructure protect reliability, and UX ensures user value.
